Press Cmd K (Ctrl K on Windows and Linux) on any page for a command palette, and question mark to see every shortcut that works where you are. G then a letter jumps to a page, slash focuses the page's search, Alt Shift L switches the theme, and on a workflow E, D and R open the editor, deploy and run. On the canvas, Cmd S saves, Cmd A selects every step, Esc clears the selection, and F, plus, minus and 0 fit and zoom.
