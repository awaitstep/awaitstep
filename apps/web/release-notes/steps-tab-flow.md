The Steps tab now draws the workflow as a rail, with a fork for a branch's arms and the flow continuing where they rejoin. Adding a step has a quick path: name it and place it after or before a step. Branches, loop bodies and try/catch arms open a map where a + on the flow picks the spot, with a ghost showing where it lands.
